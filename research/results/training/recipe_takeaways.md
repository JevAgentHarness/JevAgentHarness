## T2. Labels

**Takeaway.** Label with the environment's oracle where it exists and add a teacher's soft distribution on top (0.3 gold + 0.7 Jev, gold one-hot where the teacher's argmax is wrong); the student then exceeded its teacher on the same states.

**Label.** registered for 'no more than 5 points below Jev on C' (S2-3); the positive difference is descriptive; v1-dev differences are development numbers.

**What the records support.** Supported: the student beat the teacher on v1 dev and on C. By construction every training target's argmax is the gold option (the gold fallback covers the rows where Jev's argmax is wrong), so the teacher shaped the soft mass and never the target's top option; a student above its teacher is what this rule allows. Not supported: which label source carries the gain (no gold-only or teacher-only run exists).

## Not supported by the records

- That recovery states, rather than data size, task setting or labels, explain the v0 -> v1 gain (confounded).
- That teacher soft labels beat gold-only labels, or the reverse (no label-source run).
- That GRPO improves success or calibration beyond a temperature fit (saturated slice; C ECE matched by temperature-scaled SFT; v2 GRPO lowered stress success).
- That on-policy distillation helps once the student is more accurate than the teacher (it lowered accuracy in both tracks).
- Any data-size curve: only two training sets (v0, v1) exist.
- Any claim outside the JevGym generator beyond the tau2 reference agreement.
