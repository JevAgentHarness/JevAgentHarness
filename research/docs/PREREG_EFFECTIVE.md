> **NI decision rule (A6.21 (a)).** Every NI row (H0: P(loss) − P(win) ≥ m on n paired binary outcomes) is decided by one ordering of all (wins W, losses L) outcomes: s̃, the monotone hull of the log nominal p of the A6.14 switching rule (Tango's one-sided score p when W ≥ 1 and W + L ≥ 5, else P[Bin(n, m) ≤ L]); the decisive p = min{1, γ + U}, with U a certified upper bound (branch and bound with a Hölder bound, tolerance max(1e-7, 1e-3 · sup)) on sup over the boundary nuisance w in the (1 − γ) Clopper–Pearson set from the discordant count of P_w[s̃ ≤ s̃_obs], γ = 1e-4 (`metrics.ni_certified_p`, `rule = bb_ordered`); it rejects iff p ≤ its Holm level. Monotone tails and a coupling make the boundary point with the same discordance least favourable, so P(p ≤ α) ≤ α at every n, level and null point. Tango's asymptotic p, A6.17's p_U, the exact loss-count p, the loss-rate bound 1 − α^{1/n} at zero discordance and the paired task bootstrap are descriptive. Evidence (a check, not the argument): `results/reviews/2026-10-02/ni_exact_size_r8.md` (exact summation: ≤ 0.997 α at every Holm level for n ∈ {149, 300, 500, 1000, 1114}, ≤ 0.66 α inside the null) and `ni_power_exact_r8.md` (exact power with feasibility labels and two-family conjunctions, A6.21 (c)). C7 decides on majority pass over the 3 trials (A6.14, A6.16 (e)).
